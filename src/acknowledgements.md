# Acknowledgements

Crypto and decentralization have always been things I've been passionate about, but this book didn't turn out quite the way I wanted it to. Partly because of my procrastination, but also because I kept asking myself, "Why write a book when AI can write it?" That mindset made it even harder to be happy with what I was creating.

But here it is. I've decided to finally publish it because I've come to accept that it will never be perfect, and that's okay. I've learned to accept it for what it is.

This book is more of a history book, exploring the origins of crypto and its potential future in our society and everyday lives, rather than a technical textbook. It's meant to be an introductory book for anyone curious about crypto and decentralization who wants a place to start.

As I mentioned in the introduction, AI has helped me with some aspects of the book, mainly formatting and spell-checking. However, the content and opinions are entirely my own. If any information in the book is incorrect, that's my responsibility, and I take full responsibility for it.

Finally, I'd like to thank every developer in this space who is building the tools and infrastructure for a new and better internet. Thank you for doing this because you believe in the values behind it, not simply out of greed or in pursuit of money.
